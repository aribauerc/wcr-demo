                      Estimates based on assumptions; confirmed by a 2-week time study. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed savings: the time per case is checked against your real case data before anything is promised. Time saved is not the same as cash saved: it turns into money when it avoids a hire or lets the same team handle more cases.
                      The assumptions behind these numbers
                      • Secretary time per case today: 8 hours (onboarding 0.5, NYCIRB 0.25, coverage research 1, broker requests and follow-ups 2, portal 0.5, filing 0.75, review and notes 3).
                      • Owner time per case today: 4.75 hours (review 2, dispute preparation 1.5, follow-ups 1, invoicing 0.25).
                      • A case stays active about 6 months, so new cases per month = active cases ÷ 6.
                      • Share of time removed after Day 1-30: secretary 25%, owner 15%. After Day 31-60: secretary 50%, owner 35%.
                      • One full-time person ≈ 167 hours a month; one owner work day = 8 hours.
